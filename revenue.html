Revenue Model

IAA monetization works best when placement strategy is treated like product design.

Zenobig approaches IAA monetization as an operating system made of placement strategy, mediation setup, retention protection, reward timing, and session tolerance. The point is not to maximize interruptions. The point is to preserve enough product value for revenue to stay durable.

Revenue 01 Placement strategy

Ads appear where intent naturally pauses rather than where they cause the most immediate damage.

Revenue 02 Mediation readiness

Adapters, fill behavior, and reporting expectations are considered before launch.

Revenue 03 Retention protection

Session pacing stays readable so users still understand the product after monetization begins.

IAA Notes When ad logic is clearer, the product feels less like a trap and more like a business.

Zenobig prefers fewer, better-placed interruptions to noisy short-term gains that collapse trust. Good IAA monetization behaves like a revenue model, not a panic reflex.
