Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Zenobig mobile software and related public services.

This Privacy Policy describes how Zenobig may collect, use, store, and protect information when users access our mobile software, related support channels, and public-facing studio pages.

Section 01

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, basic technical information about device and app behavior, and limited operational data needed to run and support our products.

Section 02

How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate our products, respond to support requests, improve stability, understand product usage, and maintain a clear public support process.

Section 03

IAA Advertising and Analytics

Some Zenobig products may rely on IAA Advertising and Analytics providers to serve ads, measure performance, improve revenue systems, and understand product behavior.

Section 04

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party infrastructure for analytics, error tracking, customer support, advertising, mediation, and technical operations. Those providers may process limited data according to their own policies.

Section 05

Device Permissions

Where a product requests device permissions, the permission should relate to an obvious feature or operational need. Users can also manage permissions through device settings.

Section 06

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as it is reasonably needed to provide services, satisfy operational obligations, resolve disputes, or comply with legal requirements.

Section 07

International Transfers

Because service providers may operate in multiple regions, information may be processed or stored outside the country where a user is located.

Section 08

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights related to access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection. We will review reasonable requests submitted through our public contact routes.
