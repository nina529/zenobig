Support Guidance

Support Guidance for Zenobig products and public inquiries.

For software-related assistance, please contact the support team with the product name, device details, and a short description of the issue so the report can be understood quickly.

Response Principle Useful support starts with readable issue reports and public routes that do not disappear.
