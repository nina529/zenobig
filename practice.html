Practice Notes

Zenobig works like a release desk, not like a generic creative studio.

The practice is built around mobile software delivery, IAA-aware product operations, and a visible public support layer. We prefer tighter product loops, clearer use cases, and release surfaces that still make sense after launch day.

Note 01 Scope with discipline

We cut features until the product can explain itself in one session.

Note 02 Build with maintenance in mind

Support and policy obligations are considered while the product is being shaped.

Note 03 Monetize with tolerance in mind

IAA works better when the session survives long enough to matter.

Studio Logic Public trust is not the layer after the product. It is one of the product layers.

Zenobig treats support, business routing, and privacy publishing as live infrastructure because mobile software is judged by how it behaves in public, not just by how it behaves in a build.

Working Method

Product shape first

We define the real use case, the shortest credible launch scope, and the points where monetization might distort the product.

Working Method

Release architecture second

Store text, support routes, privacy pages, and contact signals are aligned before public review loops begin.

Working Method

Revenue discipline third

IAA is tuned around placement timing, user patience, reward logic, and mediation readiness.

Working Method

Public upkeep always

The visible business layer should feel maintained so the studio never reads like a disposable shell.
